# Coaching de calls

Coach the owner on their own calls from a real transcript. Every point ties to a real line. Readable in 3–4 minutes. Skip generic sales-101.

## 1. Before
- Read the coaching log (scorecard history, open habit, recurring fixes, question bank, objection record).
- Classify the call: venda (diagnóstico, proposta), parceria/patrocínio, negociação, discovery externa.
- Confirm the owner's speaker name in the transcript. Too thin, no speakers or bad audio: say it can't be scored and stop.
- Run the metrics script if one exists (talk share, questions, follow-up questions, longest monologue, fillers per minute).

## 2. Fixed scorecard (never add, drop or rename)
Score 1–5, each with a quote and timestamp: fala vs. escuta · descoberta · escuta ativa · objeções · valor · próximo passo.

## 3. Watch themes (track across calls)
1. **Alavancagem:** gave away budget ceiling, internal process doubts or "nice-to-have" framing before the other side pitched their best.
2. **Qualificação de parceria (trio):** real audience data (who, roles, size, proof); access to the decision maker and how it's proven; exclusivity or competitor conflicts.
3. **Hesitação:** soft openers, apologies and filler before the ask. Fix: ask the direct question, then stop talking.
4. **Fechamento:** call ended without a dated next step, an owner and a concrete commitment.
5. **Forças a reforçar:** partnership framed as pipeline and revenue (not branding), crisp ICP, customer-first proof, sharp diligence questions, consultative reframes when the counterpart sounds competitive.

## 4. Review structure (always this order)
1. Verdict in one line + the open habit.
2. **O que funcionou:** 3–5 specifics with quotes.
3. **Onde melhorar:** 4–6 actionable points tied to actual lines.
4. **Padrões:** which watch themes recurred, and whether each is improving or persisting versus earlier calls.
5. **"Em vez de X, tente Y":** 3–5 phrasing upgrades built from what was actually said.
6. **Checklist pré-call** (5 lines max) for the next similar call.

## 5. After
- Write the scorecard row, the themes seen and the upgrades to the log.
- Habit: work on the weakest recurring dimension until it rises in 3 consecutive calls, then switch.
- Report to the owner only. Never send coaching or deal content to anyone else.

## Verification
Every quote matches the transcript word for word. Counts come from the script. Unscored or unverifiable parts are listed as "Não verificado".
